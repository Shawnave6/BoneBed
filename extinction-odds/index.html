Rules

How to Play

Goal. Reach the score to beat before you run out of plays. Each act has 3 blinds and a final blind, and there are 2 acts.

Your hand. You hold creatures. Tap them in the order you want them placed, left to right. The first tap is slot 1.

Effects. Each card has a Habitat, a Diet and an effect. Effects look at neighbors (left, right, both, or 2 cards away), at being placed first or last, or at the whole row. They give +score or +mult.

Scoring. Row total = (card scores + bonus score) × (1 + mult).

Plays. A blind gives you plays, and a final blind gives . After each play your whole deck is reshuffled and you get a new hand. Your play totals add up.

Discards. You have discards per blind. Tap cards to mark them, then confirm to draw the same number. You can only discard as many cards as are left in the deck to draw.

Rewards. Beat a blind to pick 1 of 3 cards to add to your deck. Beat a final blind to pick 3 of 5, with Uncommon as the lowest rarity. You can reroll a reward time.

Diets. Carnivore and Herbivore are opposites. Omnivores trigger off either, but never count for Carnivore or Herbivore checks.

Sorted by rarity, not by draw order.

Quit this run?

Quitting removes your progress. Your deck, cleared blinds and rewards will be lost, and you will go back to the main menu.
